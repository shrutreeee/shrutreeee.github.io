Hi! I'm a final-year BS-MS student at IISER Mohali, majoring in Biology with a minor in Data Science. I'm currently doing my Master's thesis in the Ramm Lab at the Max Planck Institute for Biology (Friedrich Miescher Laboratory) in Tübingen, where I tinker with self-organizing protein systems. Before this, I hopped around ecology and evolution (with a little developmental biology on the side), working across scales and following whatever caught my interest. Honestly, I'm still figuring out where I'm headed and how to get there, but I'm enjoying picking up tools and skills along the way.

I'm not quite sure were this website is going to go, but for now it's more of a personal playground (or my treehouse:), where I hope to pen down what I'm doing and hopefully _branch_ into more science writing or whatever else catches my eye. (And maybe writing things down here will give me just enough motivation to keep at it.

Still under construction: mostly a space for me now:)
